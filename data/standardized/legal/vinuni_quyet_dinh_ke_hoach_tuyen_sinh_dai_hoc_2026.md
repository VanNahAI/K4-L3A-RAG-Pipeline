# Decision on the Issuance of the 2026 Undergraduate Admissions Plan

## Decision on the Issuance of the 2026 Undergraduate Admissions Plan of VinUniversity

- Organization: Ministry of Education and Training - VinUniversity
- Document Number: 576a/2025/QĐ-VUNI
- Date of Issuance: Hanoi, September 15th, 2025
- Signer: Tan Yap Peng, Provost

Pursuant to Official Dispatch No. 2457/BGDĐT-GDĐH dated May 19, 2025 of the Minister of Education and Training providing guidance on university and college admissions;
Pursuant to Circular No. 08/2022/TT-BGDĐT dated June 6, 2022, issued by the Ministry of Education and Training, promulgating the Regulation on university and college admissions for Preschool Education; and Circular No. 06/2025/TT-BGDĐT dated March 19, 2025 amending and supplementing a number of articles in the Regulations on university and college admissions for Preschool Education promulgated together with Circular No. 08/2022/TT-BGDĐT dated June 06, 2022 of the Minister of Education and Training;
Pursuant to Circular No. 03/2022/TT-BGDĐT dated January 18, 2022, and Circular No. 10/2023/TT-BGDĐT dated April 28, 2023, issued by the Minister of Education and Training, regulating the determination of admission quotas for undergraduate, master's, and doctoral programs, as well as college admissions quotas for Preschool Education;
Pursuant to Decision No. 1957/BGDĐT-GDĐH on the Guidelines for Undergraduate Admissions and College Admissions for Preschool Education;
Pursuant to the VinUniversity Undergraduate Admissions Regulations and the direction of the Chairperson of the Undergraduate Admissions Council;
Based on the proposal of the Head of Student Admissions;

### DECIDES

- **Article 1.** Issuance of the 2026 Undergraduate Admissions Plan of VinUniversity.
- **Article 2.** The Admissions Plan will be updated, adjusted, and supplemented based on new information and will be publicly announced before the application period, in compliance with the current admissions regulations.
- **Article 3.** The Head of Student Admissions and relevant units are responsible for implementing this Decision.

### II. ADMISSION FOR FULL-TIME UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMS

#### 1. Admission Candidates
Candidates who meet the eligibility requirements for undergraduate admission in accordance with the current Admission Regulations.
*Notes:* Candidates who are foreign nationals and candidates who are Vietnamese citizens who have completed a high school program abroad or an international high school program in Viet Nam, and hold a High School Graduation Diploma or an equivalent qualification, are required to complete the procedure for recognition of equivalent high school level in Viet Nam. This must be done in accordance with Circular No. 13/2021/TT-BGDĐT dated April 15, 2021, of the Ministry of Education and Training on the recognition of degrees granted by foreign educational institutions.

#### 2. Admission Methods
Admission Method: Code 500 (Other methods).
The VinUniversity admissions process is designed to select talented students using the A.A.C.C. Model with:
- **(1) Outstanding Abilities:** Outstanding non-academic skills: strong analytical and problem-solving skills, quick learning capability, imagination skills or excellence in language, sports, arts, and other skills and competencies that are proven determinants of success.
- **(2) Strong Aspirations:** An understanding of major issues in the local community, society, Vietnam, and the world, and a strong aspiration to find ways to solve them.
- **(3) Creativity:** The ability to have a different mindset, high curiosity to discover the root cause of issues, manage and adapt to change, and be open-minded with ideas or solutions that do not yet exist.
- **(4) Commitment:** A strong belief in what one can do and the ability, determination, and strong mental and physical concentration to pursue one's goals.

#### 3. Rules on Converting Equivalent Admission Thresholds and Scores
The conversion is based on: Academic Quality Index (academic achievements, high school academic records, results from international certificate examinations such as SAT, ACT, A-Level, AP, and others); Non-Academic Quality Index (demonstrating the A.A.C.C attributes); and Interview Results.
The table for converting equivalent admission scores will be updated after the results of the National High School Graduation Examination are released, in accordance with the general regulations and guidelines of the Ministry of Education and Training (MOET).
For Health Science Programs (Medical Doctor and Bachelor of Nursing): The minimum entry quality assurance threshold shall be applied in accordance with the regulations of the Ministry of Education and Training. Candidates admitted to the Medical Doctor program who do not yet meet the requirements for foundational knowledge in Biology and/or Chemistry at the High School level are required to participate in the corresponding supplementary modules before commencing the main curriculum.

#### 5. Entry Requirements for Language Proficiency
- **5.1. English Proficiency for All Candidates:**
  As the medium of instruction at VinUniversity is English, all admitted candidates must demonstrate English proficiency before August 30th of the corresponding academic year. The minimum requirement is IELTS (Academic) overall score of 6.5 (with no band score below 6.0) or equivalent. Results are accepted if obtained within 2 years and valid until May 15th of the admission year.
- **5.2. Vietnamese Language Proficiency for International Students in Health Sciences:**
  International students enrolled in the Doctor of Medicine (MD) Program must demonstrate Vietnamese language proficiency at Level A2 upon enrollment. VinUniversity will administer a Vietnamese language placement test upon enrollment.

#### 6. Admission Organization and Timeline
- **Application Submission Period:** October 15, 2025 – August 15, 2026 (organized into 3 rounds):
  - Early Round (Round 1): October 15, 2025 – January 15, 2026.
  - Regular Round (Round 2): February 15, 2026 – May 15, 2026.
  - Rolling Round (Round 3): June 15, 2026 – August 15, 2026.
- **Interview Period (Expected):** On a rolling basis until August 30, 2026.
- **Announcement of Admission Results (Expected):** Before September 5, 2026.
- **Confirmation of Enrollment:** Before August 30, 2026 (in accordance with MOET guidelines).
- **Supplementary Admission Review:** During September – October 2026 (if necessary).

#### 7. Priority Policies
Applied in accordance with VinUniversity's current admission regulations and MOET guidelines.

#### 8. Application Fee
- Fee: 2,000,000 VND per application/candidate for those who advance to the interview round.
- Fee waiver applies to: candidates participating in admission events or academic/extracurricular competitions organized by the University/Colleges, or other cases approved according to specific recruitment campaigns.
- Virtual filtering registration fee: applied in accordance with MOET regulations.
